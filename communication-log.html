Record-keeping

Communication Log

Write down every call and every letter, as it happens. A collector's call center can have dozens of different reps — they won't all tell you the same thing, and a written record of who said what is the only way to prove it later.

Why this matters

This isn't a legal requirement, just sound practice: it's what turns "they said" into something you can actually prove.

Why it works →
  • If two representatives give you conflicting information — one says the debt is valid, another says it was already settled — your log is what shows the inconsistency.
  • A representative who makes a threat or a false statement on a recorded or logged call is evidence toward an FDCPA claim, even if the company later denies it happened.
  • If a dispute ever goes to a regulator, an attorney, or a court, "I have no idea when or who" is a much weaker position than a dated, specific record.
